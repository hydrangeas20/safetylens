## Trade-offs (for decision DEC-1 in docs/DECISIONS.md)

- **Compact** costs 5/12 of the full design. It estimates each effect only at the baseline levels (terse wording, no cue, no pretext) and cannot detect interactions, e.g. whether the cue matters only when a pretext is present.
- **Full** reuses every response in several contrasts, which usually raises power per item, and it estimates effects averaged over the other factors plus interactions. Its estimand differs from the compact one if interactions exist.
- Both designs hold the core request constant by construction (`safetylens.items`).
- **Assumption that favours the full design:** responses to different conditions of the same item are simulated as independent given the item intercept. If items also differ in how strongly they respond to a factor (random slopes), the extra matched pairs carry less independent information and the full design's advantage shrinks. The pilot should estimate this before the design is frozen.
- Null calibration (checked with 20,000 replications at alpha = 0.05/12): exact McNemar is conservative; the Wilcoxon test on item scores holds its nominal level.
